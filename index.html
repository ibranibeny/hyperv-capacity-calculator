Verified guidance

Production baseline

  • Use ReFS CSV volumes for performance-sensitive Hyper-V VMs.
  • Keep four-drive equivalent reserve for immediate in-place repair.
  • For four-node S2D, use RDMA-capable 25 GbE or faster with redundant links.
  • Use SET, QoS, VLAN isolation, SMB Multichannel, and validate the full cluster.
  • Choose validated hardware; align server, NIC, driver, and firmware versions.

Not assessed

Measure before approval

  • NVMe IOPS, latency, endurance, and power-loss protection.
  • NUMA boundaries, CPU generation, hyper-threading, and peak CPU behavior.
  • Switch fabric, RDMA mode, DCB/PFC for RoCE, and network oversubscription.
  • Backup, checkpoints, replicas, disaster recovery, licensing, and growth outside VM disks.
